# Supply Chain Performance Analytics – GlamourGlow Cosmetics

## Project Overview

GlamourGlow Cosmetics is an imaginary fast-scaling beauty brand operating
across B2C and B2B channels.

This Tableau project analyzes the company's supply chain performance
across inventory, order fulfillment, suppliers, transportation, and
operational costs.

The objective is to transform fragmented supply chain data into an
interactive analytics dashboard that provides operational visibility
and supports data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor inventory health across warehouses and products
- Identify stockout and overstock situations
- Analyze order fulfillment and delivery performance
- Benchmark suppliers based on lead time, defects, and cost
- Evaluate transportation and carrier efficiency
- Analyze supply chain cost drivers
- Provide a centralized operational dashboard

## Project Modules

### 1. Inventory Health Analysis

Analyzes inventory levels across warehouses and products to identify
understocked and overstocked SKUs.

### 2. Order Fulfillment & Delivery

Analyzes delivery time, order status, fulfillment performance, and
regional delivery trends.

### 3. Supplier Performance

Compares suppliers based on:

- Average Lead Time
- Defect Rate
- Unit Cost
- Supplier Reliability

### 4. Shipping Efficiency

Evaluates:

- Carrier Performance
- Route Efficiency
- Delivery Success Rate
- Shipping Duration
- Fuel Cost

### 5. Supply Chain Cost Analysis

Analyzes:

- Manufacturing Cost
- Logistics Cost
- Miscellaneous Cost
- Return Cost
- Total Operational Cost
